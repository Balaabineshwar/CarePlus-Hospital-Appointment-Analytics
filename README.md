CarePlus Hospital Appointment Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end healthcare analytics project built to evaluate hospital operational performance, patient flow, departmental efficiency, and financial revenue across 8 regional branches.


 Repository Structure
dashboard - Contains the final executive Excel workbook (CarePlus Hospital Appointment Analytics.xlsx).
datasets- Raw and secondary data sources (Appointments, Doctors, Departments, Holidays).
presentation- Project presentation slides summarizing key findings and business recommendations.


Key Project Highlights & Findings
Financial Performance: Generated ₹6,71,650 in total revenue across 808 appointment records.
Operational Efficiency: The average waiting time was 26.43 minutes. Cardiology had the highest average waiting time, at approximately 51 minutes.
Appointment Trends: April recorded the highest appointment volume, with 144 appointments, and the highest monthly revenue, at ₹1,20,700. Sunday was the busiest day, with 127 appointments.
Department & Location Performance: Neurology generated approximately ₹1,13,100 in revenue, while Trichy recorded the highest appointment volume, with 117 appointments.
Appointment Status Analysis: Out of 808 appointments, 526 were completed, 149 were cancelled, and 133 were no-shows.


Business Questions Addressed
1. Departmental workload and volume distribution.
2. Individual physician patient load tracking.
3. Departmental waiting time bottlenecks and cancellation rates.
4. Hospital branch revenue and patient satisfaction scoring (averaging 3.92 / 5.0).
5. Holiday calendar impact on emergency-only vs. normal operations.
